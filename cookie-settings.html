Cookies

Cookie Settings.

Understand how apexstacktechnologies.com uses cookies and similar technologies, and learn how you can manage your preferences to control your browsing experience.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and supply information to the site owners. Cookies allow a website to recognise your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language, login details, and other settings.

In addition to cookies, apexstacktechnologies.com may use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage to collect and store information about how you interact with our site and services. Throughout this policy, we refer to all of these technologies collectively as "cookies."

How We Use Cookies

ApexStack uses cookies for a variety of purposes to enhance your experience on our website. These purposes include ensuring the site functions correctly, understanding how visitors use our pages, remembering your preferences, and delivering relevant content and advertisements. We categorise the cookies we use into four distinct groups, each serving a specific function as described below.

Cookie Categories

a. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the basic functionality of apexstacktechnologies.com. They enable core features such as page navigation, access to secure areas of the site, and form submissions. Without these cookies, the website cannot function properly. Strictly necessary cookies do not collect personal information for marketing purposes and are always active. You cannot opt out of these cookies because the site would not operate correctly without them.

b. Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with apexstacktechnologies.com by collecting and reporting information anonymously. These cookies allow us to measure the number of visitors, see which pages are most popular, and understand how users navigate through the site. The insights we gain from analytics cookies help us improve the structure, content, and performance of our website. We use tools such as Google Analytics to gather this data. All information collected by analytics cookies is aggregated and does not directly identify individual users.

c. Functional Cookies

Functional cookies enable apexstacktechnologies.com to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, more personalised features. For example, these cookies may remember your preferred language, region, or display settings so that you do not have to re-enter them each time you visit. Functional cookies may also be used to provide services you have requested, such as watching a video or commenting on a blog post. The information collected by these cookies may be anonymised and they cannot track your browsing activity on other websites.

d. Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies are used to track visitors across websites with the purpose of displaying advertisements that are relevant and engaging for the individual user. These cookies record your visit to apexstacktechnologies.com, the pages you have visited, and the links you have followed. We use this information, together with information provided by third-party partners, to make our advertising more relevant to your interests. Marketing cookies may also be used to limit the number of times you see an advertisement and to help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, apexstacktechnologies.com uses cookies set by third-party services that appear on our pages. These third-party providers help us analyse site traffic, understand user behaviour, and deliver targeted content. The third-party services we currently use include:

Google Analytics — Provides detailed statistics on website traffic, user behaviour, and audience demographics. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information in an anonymous form, including the number of visitors, where they have come from, and the pages they visited. For more information, visit Google's privacy policy.

LinkedIn Insight Tag — Enables us to understand the professional demographics of our visitors, measure the effectiveness of our LinkedIn advertising campaigns, and deliver more relevant content to LinkedIn users who visit our site. For more information, visit LinkedIn's cookie policy.

HubSpot — Powers our marketing automation, lead tracking, and content personalisation. HubSpot cookies help us understand which pages you visit, how you arrived at our site, and whether you have interacted with our forms or downloadable content. For more information, visit HubSpot's privacy policy.

Managing Your Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. There are several ways you can manage your cookie preferences:

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically find these options in the "Settings," "Preferences," or "Privacy" section of your browser. From there, you can choose to block all cookies, accept all cookies, or receive a notification when a cookie is set so that you can decide whether to accept it on a case-by-case basis. Please note that the steps for managing cookies vary depending on which browser you use. Consult your browser's help documentation for specific instructions.

Opt-Out Links

You can opt out of specific third-party analytics and advertising cookies using the following links:

Google Analytics Opt-Out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on, available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

LinkedIn Opt-Out: Manage your LinkedIn advertising preferences at linkedin.com/psettings/guest-controls

General Advertising Opt-Out: Visit youronlinechoices.com or optout.aboutads.info to manage advertising cookies from multiple providers.

Impact of Disabling Cookies

Please be aware that disabling or blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of apexstacktechnologies.com. If you choose to reject strictly necessary cookies, some parts of the site may become inaccessible or may not function as expected. Disabling analytics or functional cookies will not prevent you from using the site, but it may reduce the quality of your experience because the site will not be able to remember your preferences or provide personalised content.
