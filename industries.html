Fintech & Payments

Payment processors, neobanks, lending platforms, and insurtech companies operate under some of the strictest regulatory frameworks in the world. ApexStack Cloud helps fintech organizations build PCI-DSS compliant cloud infrastructure that processes millions of transactions with sub-10ms latency while maintaining the security posture that regulators and customers demand.


Our fintech practice covers the full spectrum of payment infrastructure challenges: real-time fraud detection systems powered by machine learning, KYC/AML data pipelines that scale with transaction volume, zero-trust network architectures that satisfy PCI-DSS Level 1 requirements, and FinOps frameworks that keep cloud costs aligned with revenue growth. Whether you are migrating a legacy core banking system or building a greenfield payment gateway, our engineers bring the domain expertise to get it right.


Key capabilities:

  • ✓ PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant infrastructure design
  • ✓ Real-time fraud detection & KYC/AML pipelines
  • ✓ Payment gateway architecture & optimization
  • ✓ Multi-region disaster recovery (RPO <15min, RTO <30min)
  • ✓ FinOps cost optimization for transaction-heavy workloads

SaaS Platforms

B2B SaaS companies face a unique engineering challenge: building multi-tenant infrastructure that scales from 10 customers to 10,000 without rearchitecting, while maintaining the reliability and performance that enterprise buyers demand. ApexStack Cloud helps SaaS platforms build the cloud foundation that supports rapid growth from seed stage through IPO and beyond.


Our SaaS practice focuses on the architectural patterns that matter most for platform companies: multi-tenant isolation strategies that balance cost efficiency with data security, auto-scaling compute layers that handle unpredictable usage spikes, CI/CD pipelines that enable daily deployments without customer-facing risk, and internal developer platforms that let your engineering teams move fast without creating infrastructure debt.


Key capabilities:

  • ✓ Multi-tenant architecture design & implementation
  • ✓ Internal developer platforms (Backstage, Port)
  • ✓ Monolith-to-microservices decomposition
  • ✓ CI/CD with canary deployments & feature flags
  • ✓ SOC 2 compliance & security hardening

Digital Marketplaces & Mobility

Marketplace and mobility platforms operate in a world of real-time matching, geospatial complexity, and demand volatility that most cloud architectures are not designed to handle. ApexStack Cloud builds the event-driven, globally distributed infrastructure that powers ride-hailing platforms, logistics networks, food delivery services, and two-sided marketplaces operating at continental scale.


Our marketplace practice addresses the unique infrastructure challenges of real-time platforms: sub-second matching algorithms that pair supply with demand across millions of concurrent users, geospatial data pipelines that process location streams from mobile devices in real time, predictive auto-scaling frameworks that pre-warm capacity before demand surges, and graceful degradation strategies for markets with unreliable network connectivity.


Key capabilities:

  • ✓ Real-time matching engines & geospatial indexing
  • ✓ Event-driven architectures (Kinesis, Kafka, EventBridge)
  • ✓ Multi-region deployment & intelligent traffic routing
  • ✓ Predictive auto-scaling for surge demand
  • ✓ Offline-capable architectures for emerging markets
