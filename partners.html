Cloud Platforms

Technologies We Use.

ApexStack leverages all major cloud platforms to architect, build, and operate multi-cloud environments that align with your business objectives and compliance requirements.

Amazon Web Services
Cloud Platform

ApexStack brings deep competency across AWS compute, storage, databases, machine learning, and serverless architecture. Our AWS-certified engineers deliver migration, modernization, and managed services at enterprise scale, helping clients get the most out of the AWS ecosystem.

Microsoft Azure
Cloud Platform

ApexStack leverages Microsoft Azure to deliver managed services, hybrid cloud integration, and enterprise-grade security for our clients. Our team holds specializations in infrastructure, data and AI, and digital app innovation, ensuring your Azure workloads run with maximum efficiency.

Google Cloud
Cloud Platform

Our expertise in Google Cloud spans data analytics, Kubernetes, and application modernization on GCP. ApexStack helps organizations leverage BigQuery, Anthos, and Vertex AI to turn raw data into actionable intelligence, with deep experience in data management, infrastructure, and machine learning.

Red Hat OpenShift
Container Platform

ApexStack specializes in deploying and managing Red Hat OpenShift container platforms for enterprises requiring hybrid cloud flexibility. Our certified architects design resilient, portable container orchestration solutions that run consistently across on-premises, public cloud, and edge environments.

Kubernetes & CNCF
Cloud-Native Technology

ApexStack has deep expertise in Kubernetes and the cloud-native ecosystem, including tools like Prometheus, Envoy, Istio, and Helm. Our platform engineers build production-grade Kubernetes clusters, implement service mesh architectures, and establish GitOps workflows that automate deployment pipelines at scale.

HashiCorp
Infrastructure Tools

ApexStack delivers infrastructure-as-code excellence through HashiCorp tools including Terraform, Vault, Consul, and Nomad. Our teams automate provisioning across multi-cloud estates, implement zero-trust security models with secrets management, and establish service networking that scales with your organizational growth.
