What We Do

Our Services.

Comprehensive cloud engineering solutions that take your organization from strategy through execution and beyond. We work with you at every stage of your cloud journey to deliver measurable business outcomes.

Strategy & Advisory

Cloud Strategy.

Every successful cloud initiative begins with a clear strategy. Our advisory teams work alongside your leadership to define the right path forward, aligning technology decisions with business objectives and organizational readiness.


⚙

Cloud Transformation

We assess your current IT landscape, identify workloads ready for the cloud, and build a prioritized transformation roadmap tailored to your business goals. Our approach covers total cost of ownership analysis, risk assessment, and organizational impact planning to ensure your cloud journey delivers real value from day one. Whether you are starting fresh or optimizing an existing multi-cloud environment, our architects design a strategy that balances speed, cost, and innovation.

★

Cloud Training & Enablement

Technology alone does not drive transformation -- your people do. ApexStack delivers hands-on training programs, certification bootcamps, and embedded coaching designed to upskill your teams on AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. From executive workshops that demystify cloud economics to deep-dive engineering labs on infrastructure as code, we equip every level of your organization with the knowledge and confidence to operate effectively in the cloud.

☰

Cloud Readiness Assessment

Before committing resources, you need clarity on where you stand. Our Cloud Readiness Assessment evaluates your infrastructure maturity, application portfolio, security posture, and team capabilities against industry benchmarks. We deliver a detailed scorecard with actionable recommendations, helping you understand the gaps, dependencies, and quick wins that will shape your cloud adoption timeline and investment priorities.

Build & Move

Foundation & Migration.

Establish a rock-solid cloud foundation and migrate your workloads with confidence. Our engineers handle the heavy lifting so you can focus on running your business while we architect the infrastructure that powers it.


☁

Cloud Foundations

A well-architected landing zone is the cornerstone of every reliable cloud environment. ApexStack designs and deploys production-ready cloud foundations that include multi-account structures, identity and access management, network topology, logging, monitoring, and governance guardrails. We build everything using infrastructure as code so your foundation is repeatable, auditable, and scalable from the start, whether you run on a single cloud or across multiple providers.

⇪

Cloud Migration

Moving workloads to the cloud requires more than a lift-and-shift. Our migration teams use proven methodologies to assess, plan, and execute migrations of any scale -- from a handful of virtual machines to thousands of enterprise applications. We handle rehosting, replatforming, and refactoring strategies based on what makes sense for each workload, minimizing downtime, reducing risk, and ensuring performance targets are met throughout the transition.

🔒

Cloud Security

Security is not an afterthought at ApexStack -- it is embedded into every layer of our delivery. We design and implement comprehensive cloud security frameworks covering identity management, encryption, network segmentation, threat detection, compliance automation, and incident response. Our security engineers hold advanced certifications across all major platforms and stay current with the evolving threat landscape to keep your data and workloads protected around the clock.

Transform & Innovate

Innovation & Modernization.

Unlock the full potential of the cloud by modernizing legacy systems, embracing cloud-native architectures, and leveraging AI and data to drive competitive advantage. Our engineering teams turn technical debt into strategic opportunity.


✨

Platform & App Modernization

Legacy applications constrain agility and inflate operational costs. ApexStack modernizes your platforms by decomposing monoliths into microservices, containerizing workloads with Docker and Kubernetes, and re-architecting applications to take full advantage of managed cloud services. We evaluate each application individually to determine the right modernization path, whether that means a full rewrite, incremental refactoring, or a strategic replatform to reduce overhead while preserving business logic.

⚖

AI & Data

Data is only valuable when it drives decisions. Our AI and data engineering teams build modern data platforms that unify ingestion, storage, processing, and analytics into a single coherent architecture. From data lakes and warehouses on AWS, Azure, or GCP to machine learning pipelines and generative AI integrations, we help you turn raw data into actionable intelligence. We also implement robust data governance, lineage tracking, and quality frameworks to ensure trust in every insight your teams produce.

◆

Cloud-Native Build

When the opportunity calls for something entirely new, ApexStack builds it cloud-native from the ground up. Our development teams design and deliver serverless applications, event-driven architectures, and API-first platforms using the latest services from AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. We follow twelve-factor app principles, implement automated testing and deployment, and engineer for resilience, observability, and horizontal scale so your new applications are ready for whatever growth demands.

🛠

Cloud DevOps

Speed and reliability should never be at odds. ApexStack implements DevOps practices that accelerate your delivery pipeline without sacrificing stability. We build CI/CD workflows, infrastructure as code pipelines, automated testing frameworks, and GitOps-driven deployment processes tailored to your team and technology stack. Our site reliability engineers establish service level objectives, error budgets, and incident management runbooks that keep your production systems performing at their best while your developers ship faster than ever.

Operate & Optimize

Managed Services.

Keep your cloud environment running at peak performance with our managed services. From day-to-day operations to continuous optimization, our teams act as an extension of yours, providing 24/7 expertise so you can focus on what matters most -- your business.


♸

Managed Cloud Services

Running cloud infrastructure at scale requires constant attention, deep expertise, and proactive monitoring. ApexStack provides fully managed cloud operations covering infrastructure management, patching, backup and disaster recovery, performance tuning, and 24/7 incident response across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. Our operations center staffs experienced engineers around the clock, with defined SLAs and transparent reporting that give you full visibility into the health and cost of your environment.

⚙

Application Management

Your cloud infrastructure is only as good as the applications it supports. Our application management service covers the full lifecycle of your business-critical applications, including monitoring, performance optimization, release management, bug resolution, and feature enhancements. We integrate with your existing tools and workflows, providing dedicated application support engineers who understand your codebase, your users, and your business priorities to keep everything running smoothly.

▾

Cloud FinOps

Cloud spending can spiral without the right discipline. ApexStack brings financial accountability to your cloud operations through our FinOps practice. We implement cost allocation tagging, rightsizing recommendations, reserved instance planning, anomaly detection, and chargeback models that align cloud costs with business value. Our FinOps analysts deliver monthly optimization reports and work directly with your engineering and finance teams to drive continuous savings without compromising performance or availability.

Whether you need strategic guidance, hands-on migration support, or a fully managed cloud environment, our team of experienced engineers is ready to help you move faster and build smarter.
